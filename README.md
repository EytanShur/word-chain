# Word Chain

A party game for 3+ players on one phone. One player guesses; the rest see a word and build a
question for it, one word each, then slam the buzzer. Rounds can be filmed with the front camera
and shared as a "memory" with the clock and words burned in.

**Play:** https://eytanshur.github.io/word-chain/ (works offline once loaded; add it to your home screen)

## Privacy

Everything runs in the browser. Scores and settings are stored only on the device. The camera and
microphone are used only during a filmed round, and the video never leaves the phone unless you share it.
